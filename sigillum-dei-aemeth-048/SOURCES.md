## 4. Historical secondary source describing the hierarchy

### "Notes Upon the Sigillum Dei Aemeth"

*The Round Robin*, Vol. 2, No. 10, October 1946.  
Hosted by Borderland Sciences Research Foundation.

https://borderlandsciences.org/journal/vol/02/n10/Sigillum_Dei_AEmeth.html

**Used for:** an independent historical description of the seal's construction and hierarchy, including:

- letters and numbers around the circumference;
- seven internal symbols;
- the double heptagon divided into seven compartments per facet;
- the seven names and their derived angelic hierarchy;
- the description of successive generations of daughters and sons;
- the broader 49-angel structure associated with the Heptarchic material.

**Source-lineage note:** this 1946 article itself cites Dee/Kelley material and the later Casaubon publication *A True & Faithful Relation*.

## 5. How these sources were used in the analysis

The analysis that accompanied the capture treated the Sigillum as a historically documented **symbolic transformation system** and compared its mechanics to modern computational concepts.

The historical basis for that comparison came from the sources above:

```text
letters / numbers
      ↓
addressed positions
      ↓
defined traversal or arithmetic rule
      ↓
derived names
      ↓
geometric placement
      ↓
further derived relationships / hierarchies
```

The modern terms used in the analysis, such as **grammar**, **lookup/transform system**, **graph generator**, and **symbolic virtual machine**, were interpretive descriptions supplied during analysis. They are not terminology used by Dee, Kelley, Peterson, Jones, or the 1946 *Round Robin* article.

## 7. AAOTH → Mercury → Hod → Aaron correspondence chain

This connection was identified while resolving the historical/computational mapping for the class-library design.

### AAOTH → Mercury

The 1946 *Round Robin* table assigns **Aaoth** to **Mercury**:

https://borderlandsciences.org/journal/vol/02/n10/Sigillum_Dei_AEmeth.html

**Used for:** the first edge in the transform chain.

### Mercury → Hod

David Richard Jones notes the standard Hermetic-Qabalistic attribution of **Mercury to Hod**:

https://hermetic.com/jones/on-the-formulae-of-the-unicursal-hexagram

Aaron J. Leitch likewise describes **Hod as the sphere of Mercury**:

https://www.jwmt.org/v2n13/doc.html

**Used for:** the planetary-to-sephirah transform.

### Hod → Aaron

A modern Kabbalistic correspondence source explicitly associates **Hod with Aharon/Aaron**:

https://ifdawn.com/esa/hod.htm

A Zohar translation published in *The Word* also preserves the older pairing in which **Moses corresponds with Netzach and Aaron with Hod**:

https://iapsop.com/archive/materials/word_percival/word_v6_oct_1907-mar_1908.pdf

**Used for:** the sephirah-to-biblical-archetype transform.

### Resulting transform

```text
AAOTH
  → Mercury
  → Hod
  → Aaron
```

This should be modeled as a provenance-preserving correspondence chain rather than as a direct spelling transformation.
